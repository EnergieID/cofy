The Access tab lists who may manage this community. You see it if you are a community admin.

## Giving someone access

1. On the Access tab, click **Grant access**.
2. Enter the **email** address the person logs in with.
3. Choose their **role**.
4. Click **Grant**.

They can log in right away. Until they do, their status is **Awaiting first login**; at their first login, access is tied to their account, and their status becomes **Account linked**. From then on, it stays theirs even if they change the email address of their account.

The person needs an account at your Cofy host, with a verified email address.

## Roles

| Role | May |
| --- | --- |
| Community admin | Everything in this community: its modules, resources, secrets, API tokens, and who else has access |

## Taking access away

Click **Revoke** beside the person. From then on they can no longer see or change the community.
