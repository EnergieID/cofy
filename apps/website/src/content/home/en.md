## Where Cofy comes from

Cofy is built by [EnergyID](https://www.energyid.eu), which has helped more than 150,000 households and small businesses understand their energy use since 2010.

It started in the Horizon 2020 project REScoop VPP, and is co-funded today by the Interreg North-West Europe project SmartCORE.
